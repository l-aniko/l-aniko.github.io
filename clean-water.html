Clean Water
Typographic Poster

Project Student project
Tools Adobe InDesign · After Effects
Focus Environmental awareness · Typography
Concept

Created for an environmental awareness project, the brief was to choose an environmental issue and communicate it through any visual technique.

I chose clean water and typography as the primary visual language, using repetition, scale and distortion to turn the message itself into the visual.

The poster was created for a Hungarian audience. Its central question, “Tiszta víz — lesz elég?”, translates as “Clean water — will there be enough?” The question draws attention to the future availability of one of our most essential resources.

Outcome

Selected for
public display.

The poster was selected among the project entries, exhibited publicly and later displayed at a Budapest bus stop, where it remained for several years.

The project was created at Budai Rajziskola in collaboration with Hegyvidék Municipality.
