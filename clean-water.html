01 / Visual Communication

Clean Water
Typographic Poster

Project Student project
Tools Adobe InDesign · After Effects
Focus Environmental awareness · Typography
Concept

Created for an environmental awareness project, the brief was to choose an environmental issue and communicate it through any visual technique.

I chose clean water and typography as the primary visual language, using repetition, scale and distortion to turn the message itself into the visual.

“Tiszta víz — lesz elég?”

“Clean water — will there be enough?”

Final poster
View full PDF ↗
Outcome

Selected for
public display.

The poster was selected among the project’s top entries, exhibited publicly and later displayed at a Budapest bus stop, where it remained for several years.

The project was created within an environmental awareness initiative connected to Budai Rajziskola and the Hegyvidék Green Office.
